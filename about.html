About SourdoughFails

Most sourdough content shows perfect loaves. This site is for the other ones — the dense bricks, the collapsed domes, the gummy centers, and the starters that refuse to rise.

The goal is simple: turn real mistakes into useful lessons. Every fail gets a short diagnosis and a practical recovery note so the next bake has a better chance.

This is a prototype

Right now the site is a visual shell. Photos, real stories, and a way for visitors to share their own fails will come next. The layout, colors, and page structure are here so we can tweak the look and feel before filling it with content.
