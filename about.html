About SourdoughFails

SourdoughFails is one person’s honest journey from complete novice toward consistently great sourdough bread.

It started with a bad loaf and a simple question: “What the hell happened?” That question turned into a habit of documenting the failures, the experiments, the unexpected wins, and the lessons that came with them.

The goal isn’t perfect bread for the camera. The goal is to get good enough that I can make great bread consistently — and eventually give those loaves away with pride.

What you’ll find here

Real results (good and bad).
Honest working theories.
Practical next steps.
And the occasional sarcastic observation about dough that refuses to cooperate.

If you’re also trying to figure this out, you’re in the right place.
